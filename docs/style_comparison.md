# Style comparison: STOCK AAJ OR KAL vs @Pahal_investment

Compared 25 most recent posts on each side (plus the full 800-post reference scrape from 12 Nov 16:19 IST to 14 Nov 11:05 IST, and 260 of our posts from 4–14 Nov 2026). Filing-like posts only for the close read. Wording below describes layout; none of their sentences are reused in our templates.

## What they do
- Headline is often short and loud: `COMPANY – Q2 FY27 (CONSOLIDATED)` or `COMPANY Q2 FY27 (CONSOLIDATED)`, sometimes with a judgment tag (`GOOD ✅`, `WEAK ⚠️`, `MIXED ⚖️`).
- Company name is frequently a ticker-like shout (FUSIONCX, KCPSUGIND) or Title Case plus event in caps for non-results (`Sal Automotive Ltd – COMMENCEMENT OF…`).
- Results body is a tight scorecard: Net Profit first, then Revenue, EBITDA, margin. Dash bullets, a blank line after the headline, and `₹x Cr | YoY ↑ n% | QoQ ↓ n%`.
- Direction uses ↑↓ only. Almost no hashtags (2/800). Almost no Hinglish in the posts themselves (English throughout).
- Corporate-action posts: one-line headline, a short dek, then `- ` fact bullets. No “Source” footer.
- Median length ~270–300 characters, about 9 lines (spacing included).

## What we do today
- Headline is Title Case `Company Ltd – Q2 FY27 RESULTS`, with scope on a second italic/plain line (`Consolidated`). No judgment tags. Almost never all-caps (2% of posts).
- Scorecard lines already carry the same figures and ↑↓, but as `• Revenue: ₹… ↑8.4% YoY | ↑7.3% QoQ` (Revenue first, bullet is `•`, tag after the arrow, no blank line).
- Many results and most management posts paste long press-release or legal prose, so some items run to 800–1,000+ characters.
- Almost every filing post ends with a `Source` link. No hashtags. English, not Hinglish (correct — their channel name is Hinglish, their posts are not).
- Median filing post ~236 characters when the scorecard fires; longer when prose leaks through.

## What we are changing (our own words)
- Keep Title Case `Company Ltd`. Fold scope into the headline as `Q2 FY27 (CONSOLIDATED)`. Drop the extra “RESULTS” / scope line.
- Lead the scorecard with Net Profit, then Revenue, EBITDA, margin. Use `- ` bullets, a blank line after the headline, and ` | YoY ↑ 8.4% | QoQ ↓ 2.6%`.
- Keep ↑↓ on the numbers. Do **not** add their GOOD/WEAK/MIXED tags or copy any of their sentences.
- Label the exchange PDF `Filing` instead of `Source`. Still no hashtags, still English.
- Corporate-action posts get the same chrome (blank line, dashes, Filing). We do not invent a dek or rewrite the underlying facts.
